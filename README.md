# All of Us - Add a Deceased Loved One to a Photo

Add someone who passed away to a picture without learning Photoshop. [All of Us](https://allofusphoto.com) takes the photo you treasure plus one picture of the person you miss, and shows three free previews of them in the moment. Everyone already in the photo stays exactly as they were.

## What you get

- Three free, watermarked previews per project, no account needed
- Full-resolution download plus restored color, black & white and vintage versions
- Print-ready files that look sharp at 4x6 and 5x7
- Photos stay private and are never used to train AI
